Our Purpose

Connecting Women,
Creating Impact

Sage Connection is a community united by a shared purpose: to stay connected, feel valued, and continue contributing in ways that matter.

Grounded in Support, Advice, Guidance, and Experience, Sage provides a space where women's wisdom is recognised, shared, and put into action. Together we continue to make a difference — uplifting each other and the communities we live in.

Our Founders

Who We Are

Led by women who believe deeply in the power of connection, experience, and contribution.

Director & Co-Founder

Wendy Cheney

Wendy enjoyed a long and rewarding career as a Clinical Professor in Pediatric Dentistry, where she combined clinical expertise with a passion for teaching, mentoring, and supporting the next generation of health professionals. Today, she is embracing a new direction as a co-founder of Sage Connection, a women’s community focused on connection, engagement, and purposeful contribution. Inspired by the belief that experience, wisdom, and relationships matter deeply at every stage of life, she is helping to build a welcoming space for women to form meaningful connections, share ideas, and participate in community-focused initiatives.

Community Lead & Co-Founder

Kris Faithfull

Kris is Community Lead & Co-Founder of Sage Connection, bringing a lifelong commitment to learning, leadership, and empowering others. With 25 years of experience as an educator and leader across a wide range of schools, she has dedicated her career to supporting growth, nurturing curiosity, and creating meaningful opportunities for people to thrive.

Passionate about fostering authentic connection and community, Kris leads with integrity, warmth, and purpose. She is deeply committed to supporting and uplifting women, creating spaces where people feel valued, heard, and inspired to continue making a positive difference in their communities.

Together, Wendy and Kris founded Sage Connection to create a welcoming community where experience is honoured and continues to serve the wider community.

Sage Connection provides highly practical, non-clinical assistance to a start-up small business in occupational therapy: Follow Me Therapy.
